What is it used for?

Our Bento Boxes are perfect for restaurants, cloud kitchens, and food delivery services looking for sustainable packaging solutions. These compartmentalized containers are ideal for:

  • Asian cuisine restaurants serving rice bowls and combo meals
  • Corporate catering for organized meal presentations
  • Food delivery services requiring leak-proof containers
  • Meal prep businesses offering portion-controlled meals
  • Event catering for easy serving and cleanup

Key Benefits

Made from agricultural waste and natural fibers, these boxes offer superior performance while being environmentally responsible. The multi-compartment design keeps different food items separate, maintaining flavors and presentation quality during transport.
